## Scientific interpretation
`safe` simplification uses only protected-semantics-aware identities intended to be globally valid in FastSymbolicGP. `certified` simplification may use stronger reductions, but labels them as empirical reference-set equivalence rather than global symbolic equivalence. `preset="publication"` uses safe final simplification.
